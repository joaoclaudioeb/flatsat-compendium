# FlatSat platforms: systematic mapping (2023)

Data from the systematic mapping of FlatSat platforms for small satellites, published in [Barcellos et al., IEEE J-MASS, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/JMASS.2023.3249044). The data may be expanded and corrected over time.

- [`flatsat-mapping-2023.pdf`](flatsat-mapping-2023.pdf) — summary and table of the 65 analyzed works
- [`data/flatsat_platforms.tsv`](data/flatsat_platforms.tsv) — one row per work (classification, platform features, and tests)
- [`data/references.bib`](data/references.bib) — references of the analyzed works (key = `bibkey` column)

To regenerate the PDF after editing the data, run `make`.
